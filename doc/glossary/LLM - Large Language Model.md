# LLM - Large Language Model

**LLM** (Large Language Model) is a type of artificial intelligence system that has been trained on vast amounts of text data to understand and generate human-like text. These models use deep learning architectures, typically based on transformer neural networks, to learn patterns, context, and relationships in language. LLMs can perform a wide range of natural language processing tasks including translation, summarization, question answering, code generation, and creative writing without being explicitly programmed for each specific task.

## Architecture and Training

Large Language Models are characterized by their massive scale, often containing billions or even trillions of parameters. They are trained using self-supervised learning techniques on diverse text corpora from the internet, books, and other sources. The training process enables these models to develop an understanding of grammar, facts, reasoning abilities, and even some level of common sense. Modern LLMs can be fine-tuned for specific tasks or domains, and they continue to improve as model architectures and training techniques evolve.
